# Songs in the Key of Life (1976)

**Artist:** Stevie Wonder  
**Release Year:** 1976  
**Label:** Tamla / Motown  
**Studios:** Crystal Sound (Hollywood), The Hit Factory (NYC), Record Plant (Sausalito)  
**Producer:** Stevie Wonder  

## Overview

*Songs in the Key of Life* is the towering artistic and commercial summit of Stevie Wonder's career. Spanning two LPs and a 4-track EP (*A Something's Extra*), the album debuted at #1 on the Billboard Pop Album chart and stayed there for 14 non-consecutive weeks.

## Rhythm Section & Interplay

Unlike the predominantly solo overdubbing on *Innervisions*, *Songs in the Key of Life* captured an explosive live rhythm ensemble:
- **Nathan Watts on Bass:** Delivering iconic electric bass lines that locked with kick drums (e.g. the walking chromatic riff of "I Wish" and the joyous bounce of "Sir Duke").
- **Raymond Pounds on Drums:** Flawless dynamic control and pocket propulsion.
- **Horn Sections:** Tight, punchy brass arrangements featuring Hank Redd, Trevor Lawrence, and Steve Madaio providing rhythmic punctuation.
- **Yamaha GX-1 Synthesizer:** Stevie's massive polyphonic instrument that provided lush string and brass emulations alongside his signature Rhodes and Clavinet.
