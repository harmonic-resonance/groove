# I Wish

**Artist:** Stevie Wonder  
**Album:** Songs in the Key of Life (1976)  
**Tempo:** 106.0 BPM  
**Key:** E♭ minor  
**Time Signature:** 4/4  

---

## Groove & Pocket Analysis

### The Pocket
The groove is driven by Nathan Watts' unstoppable 8-bar repeating bass motif. The drums play an assertive, rock-solid backbeat, giving the bass all the space it needs to dance between the eighth-note pulses and syncopated chromatic approach notes.

### Micro-timing & Articulation
Notice how Nathan Watts uses staccato releases on the 8th notes to leave micro-silences. The groove is defined as much by where the notes end as where they begin.

### Interlocking Rhythms
Stevie's left-hand keyboard comping doubles key chordal accents while his right hand fires off conversational fills in between vocal phrases.

## Rehearsal & Play-Along Guide
- Mute the Bass stem and practice playing Nathan Watts' walking line: focus on the staccato cutoff of the notes and dynamic consistency.
- Mute the Keyboards stem and practice funky rhythm comping over the walking bass groove.
- Loop the bridge turnaround in Audacity to master the transitional brass and rhythm section hits.
